🎀 A quick note before ordering

Read Before Purchasing

Squishies are soft, delicate products and every piece can feel or look a little different. Here are a few things to know before bringing one home ♡

💗

What to Expect

Small differences are part of the squishy experience.

🌸
Each piece may vary

Colour, shape, softness, texture and finishing may vary slightly from the photos or from another piece.

☁️
Small marks can happen

Tiny bubbles, pinholes, light wrinkles or uneven areas can naturally appear on soft squishy materials.

📦
Shipping may change the shape

A squishy may arrive slightly pressed or dented after packing. Soft foam usually improves after being left out for a while.

✨
They will not stay perfect forever

Squishies are meant to be squeezed, so some wear over time is normal — especially with frequent or rough play.

🧸

Different Squishies Feel Different

Not every squishy is made to behave the same way.

☁️

Soft & Slow-Rising

Made for that soft, satisfying squeeze. They may develop wrinkles or small foam marks with use.

🍪

Crunchy / Textured

These have extra texture or filling for a different feeling. They can be more delicate, so avoid squeezing too aggressively.

🫧

Gel / Bead / Slushy

Filled squishies can be extra satisfying but are also easier to damage if stretched, pulled or punctured.

🎀

A Few Gentle Reminders

♡

Please avoid pulling thin parts such as ears, tails, decorations or glued accessories.

♡

Product photos may look slightly different depending on lighting and your screen.

♡

Some squishies may have a factory scent when first opened. See our Squishy Care page for more info.

♡

If you prefer a specific colour or variation, please check with me before confirming your order.

💌
Purchase Policy

Please confirm carefully before ordering

♡

No change-of-mind refunds or exchanges after an order has been confirmed.

♡

Please check the product, quantity, colour/type, price and delivery method before confirming.

♡

♡
