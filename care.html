Squishy Care & Safety

A few easy things to remember before you squish ♡

Some squishies may have a strong factory smell when first opened. The smell usually becomes lighter after airing out or playing with it for a while.

👃
Please do not smell the squishy closely or directly.
🧼
Wash your hands after playing, especially before eating.
🍭
Do not put the squishy in your mouth or eat it.
🌬️
If the smell is strong, leave it in a well-ventilated area for a while.
💦
If suitable for the squishy, gently clean the surface and let it dry completely.
✨
Play, squish & have fun — but remember to keep your hands clean! 💕
