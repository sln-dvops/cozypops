Squishy Care & Safety

A few simple things to keep your squishies cute, clean and fun to play with ♡

🧸 💗 ✨ 🍰 🎀
🌸

About the factory smell

Some squishies may have a strong factory smell when first opened. The smell usually becomes lighter after airing out or playing with the squishy for a while.

👃

Don't smell closely

Please do not smell the squishy closely or directly.

🧼

Wash your hands

Wash your hands after playing, especially before eating.

🍭

Not for eating

Never put the squishy in your mouth or attempt to eat it.

🌬️

Air it out

If the smell is strong, leave it in a well-ventilated area for a while.

💦

Clean gently

If suitable for the squishy, gently clean the surface and let it dry completely.

✨

Squish gently

Play, squish and have fun — but remember that squishies can wear or tear if handled roughly.

💌 Before ordering

Please read our Important Notice

Squishies are delicate products and small imperfections can be completely normal. Please read this before confirming your purchase.
